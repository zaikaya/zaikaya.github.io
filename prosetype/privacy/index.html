Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Prosetype (“the App”), developed by Prat Tana (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles user information.

Prosetype is a private journal. Protecting your privacy is a core feature of the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data.

The App:

  • Does not require an account
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies
  • Does not access the internet or transmit data off the device
  • Does not use third-party SDKs that collect data

All journal entries and content, including any mood ratings you record, are stored locally on your device only.

2. Local Storage and Encryption

All journal data is stored exclusively on your device using a local database.

In versions of the App that offer encryption, you can protect the database with a password. The password is stored only on your device, in the system’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android’s encrypted storage). We cannot recover a forgotten password or your data.

We do not have access to your data, and your data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Export and Backup

The App allows you to export your journal entries (as a CSV file or a Prosetype backup file) and to import them again, at your request.

Exported files:

  • Are created only when you initiate an export
  • Are saved to a location you choose on your device
  • Are not accessed, transmitted, or collected by the developer

Once exported, these files are outside the control of the App.

4. Links and Email

When you tap the Privacy Policy, Terms, or Contact links in Settings, your device opens them in your browser or email app. Those apps and the websites you visit are governed by their own privacy practices.

5. Data Deletion

Deleting the App will remove all journal data stored within the App from your device.

Any files you exported outside of the App (such as CSV or backup files) are not managed by the App and will not be deleted automatically when the App is removed.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated “Last updated” date.
